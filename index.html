Measure it, then grow it

Use the test on its own to see where creativity stands, or run the full loop with the programme to grow it and prove the change.

  1. 1

    Measure

    Students take a short test, scored by AI across 8 creativity areas.

  2. 2

    Diagnose

    Reports show strengths and gaps for each student, class and school.

  3. 3

    Grow

    Students design, test their ideas in AR and 3D-print them, with activities aimed at the gaps.

  4. 4

    Measure again

    A second test shows how much each student has grown.

From an idea to something they can hold

  1. 1Design

    Students build an idea from basic shapes, on the board and in the app.

  2. 2See it in AR

    The phone turns the board into a 3D model they can look at from every side and improve.

  3. 3Print it

    The best designs go to the 3D printer, and the idea ends up in their hands.
