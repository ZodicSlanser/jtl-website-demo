Questions companies ask

Can we buy only the test?

Yes. Use the test on its own for hiring and teams, or for the children in your programmes. You can add a programme later.

Should the test decide who we hire?

No. It adds one objective measure of creative thinking. Use it alongside interviews and technical tests, as evidence behind a decision rather than the decision itself.

How does a pilot work?

We agree one team or one hiring round with you, set up the test, run it and walk you through the individual and team results.

Which ages are the children’s programmes for?

Children aged 6 to 12, with special formats for older children, such as the workshop for 12 to 14 year-olds at Autostadt.

Can we measure the impact on children?

Yes. Children take the AI-scored creativity test before and after, and you receive the results.
