Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What this page covers

This notice covers the justtolearn.com website: the booking and request forms, the Talent Compass quiz and the newsletter sign-up. The NEOMI apps and platform have their own terms, which schools and parents receive when accounts are created.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: your name, role, organisation, country, email, phone number if you add it, your child’s age if you are a parent, and your message. The Talent Compass quiz stores your answers in your own browser; if you choose to send your email with the results, we receive the email and the track scores.

How we use it

To answer your request, arrange a demo or pilot, and send you what you asked for. We do not sell your details or share them with advertisers.

Where it goes

Form requests are delivered to our team’s inbox and kept in a spreadsheet that only Just to Learn staff can open.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details you sent at any time by writing to admin@justtolearn.com.
