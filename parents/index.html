Find out what kind of creative your child is.

With NEOMI, children aged 6 to 12 design their own ideas, see them come alive in augmented reality and 3D-print them. Start with a free quiz or a creativity assessment and see where your child shines.

Take the Talent Compass quizRequest an assessment

What your child does

  1. Imagines and designs

    Your child builds an idea from basic shapes, on a board and in the app.

  2. Sees it come alive

    Augmented reality puts the design in the room, to look at from every side and improve.

  3. Holds it in their hands

    The best ideas are 3D-printed, so they become real objects.

Questions parents ask

Which ages is NEOMI for?

Children aged 6 to 12.

Does my child need tech or design experience?

No. Children learn the app as they use it. They need a phone or tablet.

Why does NEOMI use augmented reality?

AR lets children see their design as a 3D object in the real room, look at it from every side and decide what to improve.

Why does NEOMI focus on art?

Art lets children try things, make mistakes and create something unique. It is a simple way for them to experiment with and share complex ideas.

Can I buy the home programme now?

Not yet. Join the waiting list and we will contact you when it is ready.
