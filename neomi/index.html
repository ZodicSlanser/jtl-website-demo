The NEOMI Creative Thinking Test

Short creative tasks, scored by AI, with instant results and reports for every student, class, school and region. A rapid version is available when time is short. Schools, universities, ministries and companies can use it on its own or as part of the programme.

4 core skills

Fluency
How many ideas a child produces.
Flexibility
How many different kinds of ideas.
Originality
How unusual the ideas are.
Elaboration
How much detail the ideas carry.

4 extended skills

Imagination
Picturing what isn’t there yet.
Emotion
Putting feeling into the work.
Visual expression
Saying things through images.
Descriptiveness
Explaining ideas in words.

Built on STEAM

Children are surrounded by screens but rarely use them to make anything. NEOMI’s challenges mix science, technology, engineering, art and maths, so students learn to think critically, solve problems creatively and connect ideas across subjects. It’s not about memorising. It’s about exploring, experimenting and making.

  • SScience
  • TTechnology
  • EEngineering
  • AArt
  • MMaths

  • Art at the heart

    Art gives children a space to explore, express themselves and take risks without fear. They can try, make mistakes and make something of their own, and share complex ideas in a simple way.

  • Augmented reality

    Children see their ideas in 3D straight away, which makes abstract ideas easier to grasp. Because smartphones are everywhere, even in low-income areas, AR can reach almost any school.

  • Artificial intelligence

    AI scores each child’s creative thinking, recognises the shapes they build and helps match activities to their strengths, tracking progress along the way.

  • 3D printing

    The last step. Students hold their idea in their hands as a real model, link ideas to results and start to see themselves as real makers.

Built on decades of creativity research

Two traditions shape how NEOMI measures creativity, and three shape how it grows it.

How we measure

Guilford
J.P. Guilford’s model of divergent thinking, the origin of fluency, flexibility, originality and elaboration: the 4 core skills the test scores.
Torrance
E. Paul Torrance’s Tests of Creative Thinking, the most widely used creativity assessment, the model for how the test tasks are built and scored.

How we grow it

De Bono’s lateral thinking
Techniques for escaping obvious answers and reframing problems, used in activities that push for original solutions.
SCAMPER
Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse: the steps students use to redesign their own creations.
Langer’s mindfulness
Ellen Langer’s idea of noticing new things and seeing more than one perspective, used to build the habit of questioning first ideas.
