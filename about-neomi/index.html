About the app

Where ideas take shape

NEOMI is a mobile and web app that helps students turn their ideas into things they can see, explore, and build. Students design with basic geometric shapes, then view their work as a 3D model or place it in the real world with augmented reality. Seeing a design from every angle helps them judge it the way a designer judges a prototype: what works, what doesn't, and what to change next.

What is the NEOMI program
Our purpose

Creativity is a skill that grows with practice

It isn't a talent you either have or don't. NEOMI gives students a place to practice it, and to see their progress.

Imagine and build

Every activity asks students to imagine, design, test, and improve an idea, often within set limits such as a shape count or a budget.

Think like a maker

Students use real math and engineering thinking: counting, measuring, calculating cost, and working in three dimensions.

See it for real

3D views, AR, and 3D printing turn a flat drawing into a prototype students can walk around, evaluate, and hold.

1Section one

Lessons

Lessons are where students create. Each lesson takes a design from first sketch to a model they can print.

Draw shapes

Build objects from NEOMI's library of basic geometric shapes.

Colors & textures

Apply colors and textures to bring each design to life.

Size & cost

Calculate sizes and see the cost, based on the number and type of shapes, the colors, and the textures used.

3D & AR view

Rotate the model in 3D, or place it in the room around you with AR.

STL download

Export any design as an STL file and print it on a 3D printer.

Groups · distance learning

Build together, from anywhere

Up to 5 students can design one project at the same time, even when they are not in the same place. Each student uses one of the 5 AR tokens, and each token has its own unique QR code.

For young children

Less screen, more hands

To reduce screen time, younger learners build their shapes with the physical AR Kit first. When they finish, they take a photo, and NEOMI converts it into a 3D or AR object. Most of the creative work happens with their hands; the screen is only the final step.
